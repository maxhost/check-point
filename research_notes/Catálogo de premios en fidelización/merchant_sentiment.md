# Merchant sentiment on configuring loyalty rewards and marketing campaigns (cafés, bars, restaurants)

Method note: ~18 searches/fetches. Reddit could not be fetched (tool blocked for reddit.com; search engine returned no indexed Reddit threads for these queries), so first-hand merchant voices come mainly from the Square Seller Community, Shopify Community, Capterra, Trustpilot, the Loyverse community forum and an old Warrior Forum thread. Vendor content is labeled as such. Spanish-language results were almost entirely vendor marketing.

## 1. Do merchants complain about re-creating the same reward many times, or about catalog/setup complexity?

### Takeaway
No first-hand complaint was found about having to re-create the same offer repeatedly, nor about a reward catalog being too complex. The dominant merchant complaints go the other way: tools are too rigid (can't make custom rewards, can't edit an existing coupon, can't use one code across channels), and the #1 stated requirement from small café owners is speed/simplicity at the counter, not configuration power.

### Cited Findings
- Café owner wanting to edit an existing campaign coupon rather than rebuild it: "I have a birthday coupon that i'd like to edit from a $ to a %" — refuel-cafe, 2021-08-03, [Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Questions-How-To/How-do-I-edit-the-Coupon-Code-in-a-marketing-campa/td-p/32045) (merchant)
- Merchant wanting ONE reusable code across a campaign and their web store: "Square system of generating coupon codes does not work with our web back-end which requires one code...not a unique code for each customer"; and "the template does not allow me to edit the code" — HouseOfStemms, 2017-02/03, [same thread](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Questions-How-To/How-do-I-edit-the-Coupon-Code-in-a-marketing-campa/td-p/32045); another merchant replied "I am running into the same issue" (Adornkids, 2019-12-21) (merchants)
- Rigidity of reward definition (Square Loyalty, Capterra): "it won't allow you to do truly custom rewards" (Steve G., retail, 2025-06-17); "I wish there were a few more customization options for rewards" (Jared K., Food & Beverages, 2025-06-26); "the customization is somewhat limited" (Maria D., 2025-09-30) — [Capterra Square Loyalty reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/276727/Square-Loyalty/reviews/) (merchants)
- Café owner's requirement is speed, not configuration: "It needs to be EASY. When someone comes in quick for a simple hot coffee, I need to be able to quick find them"; "If I have to stop with each customer and ask them to enter their email, it will be too time consuming"; also wanted a "unit-based reward system" (stamps, not $-points) — currencysales, 2022-08-13, [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/whats-the-best-simple-pos-loyalty-program-for-a-coffee-shop/142981) (merchant)
- Loyverse community loyalty forum threads (merchant-authored titles) cluster on redemption and scoping, not on duplicating offers: "Is there a way to make products for loyalty members that they can buy with their points?" (2022-06-27), "Redeeming points" (2024-06-08), "Exclude loyalty points on selected items" (2023-10-24), "LOYALTY FOR SEPERATE ITEMS" (2023-02-24), stamp-card request (2017-09-10) — [Loyverse Community, Loyalty forum](https://loyverse.town/forum/113-loyalty/)
- Vendor claim (not merchant): "A 'free' platform requiring 5 hours per week of management time is more expensive than a $200/month platform running largely on autopilot" — [loop.fans (vendor blog)](https://loop.fans/blog/how-to-choose-restaurant-loyalty-program)
- Vendor review of Square: lacks "sophisticated campaign tools, automated win-back sequences" and no native punch-card format ("everything is converted to points") — [loop.fans (vendor, competitor)](https://loop.fans/blog/square-loyalty-program-review)

### Inferences
- The "edit the birthday coupon from $ to %" case suggests owners think of an offer as a persistent object they tweak over time (supports a reusable/editable reward entity), but the evidence is one post.
- Nothing suggests owners want to manage a catalog as a separate concept; they want few rewards, easy to edit, and fast to apply. A catalog that is invisible/lightweight (pick-or-create inline) fits the evidence better than either extreme.

### Gaps
- No Reddit/Facebook-group evidence could be retrieved; no direct quote found either praising or complaining about a reusable reward catalog.
- No merchant data on how many distinct offers a small café actually runs.

## 2. Which reward types work best for small cafés/bars (free item vs % off vs $ off vs BOGO)?

### Takeaway
Owners and platform data lean toward free item / stamp-style ("10th drink free"), with owners explicitly asking for unit-based (per-visit/per-item) rather than spend-based points. $-rewards appear in spend-based setups. No owner evidence found on BOGO.

### Cited Findings
- Coffee shop owner: "I gave a star per visit, and their 10th star gave them a free drink" with a "$5 minimum spend"; "overall it was worth it in the long run" — ryanwanner, 2025-01-09, [Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Customer-Engagement/Is-square-loyalty-worth-it/td-p/771085) (merchant)
- Ice cream shop owner running $-rewards: "one point for every dollar spent...earn a $1 reward for every ten points"; program "costing me about 1.5 cents per dollar"; loyalty customers "made up 17% of my total sales, and that 17% spent on average 93% more" — TheRealChipA, 2025-01-09, [same thread](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Customer-Engagement/Is-square-loyalty-worth-it/td-p/771085) (merchant)
- Coffee shop owner struggling to reward separate segments (bean buyers vs drink customers) — BidwellPerk, 2025-03-15, [same thread](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Customer-Engagement/Is-square-loyalty-worth-it/td-p/771085) (merchant)
- Vendor data: "Over 48% of food and drink businesses that use Square Loyalty offer a free item as their reward"; most coffee/tea sellers set up spend-based programs — [Square (vendor)](https://squareup.com/us/en/the-bottom-line/reaching-customers/coffee-shop-loyalty-program)
- Critic view (coffee-industry writer, not an owner): "Buy 10, get one free" is a 10% reward vs ~1.75% in airline programs — a heavy burden on small shops; customers game it: "order small black coffees, get their punches, then order the most expensive drink" — Ashley Rodriguez, 2023-11-09, [Boss Barista](https://bossbarista.substack.com/p/i-hate-coffee-shop-loyalty-programs)
- Merchant wish to restrict rewards: "I wish we could make it where customers can't use loyalty rewards on sale items" — Jacqueline M., retail, 2024-12-05, [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/276727/Square-Loyalty/reviews/)

### Inferences
- A free specific item (e.g., "free espresso") with a minimum-spend guard addresses the "cheap punch, expensive redemption" gaming noted by Boss Barista and ryanwanner's $5 minimum.
- Item-scoped rewards and exclusions are recurrent asks (Loyverse, Capterra, BidwellPerk).

### Gaps
- No owner-sourced comparison of BOGO vs % vs $ performance for cafés/bars; no bar-specific evidence found.

## 3. Pain points redeeming without POS integration (staff errors, fraud, tracking cost)

### Takeaway
Merchant evidence points to employee fraud with paper stamps, counter friction when looking up members, and hard-to-track costs of outstanding points. Digital + per-staff attribution is the usual answer, but friction at peak time is the owners' biggest operational worry.

### Cited Findings
- Bakery chain owner: "The problem that I am facing is not customer fraud but employee fraud." "It is really easy for them to stamp the card and get the bonus purchase to sell at retail price." — rawandrew, ~2014, [Warrior Forum](https://www.warriorforum.com/offline-marketing/923245-loyalty-punchcard-fraud.html) (merchant; old thread)
- Pub owner: the only way to bring up membership is typing phone numbers, creating friction at peak times; wishes for "swipe card or use a barcode scanner" — ChickenRacer, 2025-01-17, [Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Customer-Engagement/Is-square-loyalty-worth-it/td-p/771085) (merchant)
- Fivestars merchant (6-yr customer): "Customers treated points like a retirement system and never used them. You end up with regular customers with huge points balances" — John Strong, Dec 2024, [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.fivestars.com) (merchant) — outstanding liability / tracking cost
- Toast merchant (Capterra, summarized by search): marketing campaigns "were not clearly written", many dine-in customers "came in expecting free items", causing disruptions and financial loss — [Capterra Toast POS reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/) (merchant; exact reviewer/date not retrieved)
- Vendor claims (not merchant): a Portland café owner "going through 2,000 punch cards every 3 months but only seeing about 150 redemptions" — cited in [perkpad (vendor)](https://perkpad.io/blog/paper-punch-cards-vs-digital-loyalty-an-honest-comparison-for-cafe-owners); a product exists specifically marketed as "Stop Stamp Fraud in Your Coffee Shop with Staff Tracking" — [BaristaCard (vendor)](https://baristacard.com/en)

### Inferences
- For manual cashier redemption, per-staff attribution of stamps/redemptions and unambiguous reward wording (what exactly is free, conditions) directly address the fraud and "customers expecting free items" failure modes.
- Unredeemed-points hoarding argues for expiring or visit-based rewards rather than open-ended point balances.

### Gaps
- No quantified merchant data on staff error rates for manual redemption.

## 4. Complaints/praise on Fivestars, Square, Toast, Thanx, Stamp Me, Loyverse, Smile.io

### Takeaway
Square: praised for simplicity, criticized for rigid rewards and weak campaigns. Fivestars: pricing/contracts and stagnation. Toast: campaign confusion, lock-in. Thanx, Stamp Me, Smile.io: no usable merchant evidence found in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Square: Capterra cons on customization and data capture: "We can not capture emails for marketing purposes easily" (Richie P., 2024-11-20); wanted "specific data like birthdays at sign-up" (Rich P., 2025-06-17) — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/276727/Square-Loyalty/reviews/)
- Fivestars: "It's easier to get out of a timeshare than it is to close your never ending contract" (Initial D, May 2025); "a couple cheap tablets and a system that allows you to text your clients" (May 2025); "Tired system that never grew or developed" (John Strong, Dec 2024) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.fivestars.com); BBB complaint pattern on auto-renewal — [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/san-francisco/profile/internet-marketing-services/fivestars-loyalty-inc-1116-464125/complaints)
- Toast: loyalty only works with Toast POS; data doesn't migrate — [favecard (vendor, competitor)](https://www.favecard.co/en/blog/toast-loyalty-program/); campaign confusion quote above — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/)
- Loyverse: free, points→discount only; free-item rewards and stamp cards are recurring merchant requests — [Loyverse help](https://help.loyverse.com/help/points-discounts), [Loyverse Community](https://loyverse.town/forum/113-loyalty/)
- Stamp Me: G2 has too few reviews for insights — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/stamp-me/reviews)

### Inferences
- Across tools, reward rigidity (points-only, no item rewards, no edit) is a more common merchant complaint than setup complexity.

### Gaps
- Thanx and Smile.io (Smile is e-commerce; less relevant) merchant reviews on campaign setup were not retrieved.

## 5. Latin American / Spanish-language perspectives

### Takeaway
Only vendor content found (Square ES, Loyapp, Letstamp, Promotty, Loyicard, CoffeeSapiens); no first-hand LatAm owner commentary retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor-claimed outcome: "en un mes, más del 70% de los clientes habituales ya tenían la tarjeta en su móvil" — [Loyicard (vendor)](https://loyicard.com/como-fidelizar-clientes-en-tu-cafeteria-sin-apps-ni-tarjetas-fisicas/)
- Spanish vendor content frames the digital stamp card (QR) as the default model for bars/cafés — [Loyapp (vendor)](https://loyapp.es/tarjetas-de-sellos-digitales-5-ejemplos-de-uso-para-bares-y-cafeterias/), [Letstamp (vendor)](https://www.letstamp.com/es/como-crear-una-campana-de-tarjeta-de-sellos-para-cafeterias)

### Inferences
- The Spanish-language market vocabulary is "tarjeta de sellos" + "premio" (one reward per card), consistent with the unit/free-item preference seen in English merchant posts.

### Gaps
- No Ecuador/LatAm Facebook group, forum, or review-site merchant voices retrieved; Fidelizarte and Rappi merchant perspectives not found.
